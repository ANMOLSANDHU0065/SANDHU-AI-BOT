The story behind the workspace

Meet SANDHU AI.

A personal AI assistant project focused on making everyday learning, problem-solving and creativity more approachable.

Our purpose

Bring useful assistant experiences into one organised workspace — from learning and writing to coding and planning.

Our design values

Clarity, simplicity and thoughtful details. A calm interface should help people focus on what matters.

Created by

Built by Anmol Singh Sandhu as an evolving technology project. The goal is to keep learning, improving and building.

The range and quality of answers depend on the knowledge engine and services connected to the backend. The interface alone does not turn a rule-based chatbot into a generative AI model.
